# English practice alongside RAG

## Coaching preference

Use Chinese for lessons, exercise instructions, and technical feedback, starting with Lesson 01. Keep key English technical terms. Use English for everyday communication and learner answers. Save lessons and their learning records as Markdown files. Prioritize RAG knowledge and technical understanding. Keep lesson bodies focused on RAG concepts, exercises, and technical reviews. Put all grammar, spelling, word-choice corrections, and edited English reference answers in module 4 (English knowledge supplements) at the very bottom of each lesson document, never interleaved with the main content. Keep learner answers in their original exercise positions. Prioritize recurring or meaning-changing mistakes; avoid interrupting RAG learning.

Suggested response pattern:

1. Technical response or review.
2. When useful, add a brief English correction after the technical response in chat.
3. In saved lesson documents, place English corrections only in module 4 at the bottom.

## Initial corrections — 2026-10-19

Original:
> now i wanna learn rag and command it,you help to make an plan ,i will follow this plan to learn step by step.

Natural version:
> I want to learn RAG and master it. Please help me create a step-by-step learning plan that I can follow.

Notes:
- "Master RAG" expresses gaining strong knowledge and skill.
- Use "a plan," because "plan" begins with a consonant sound.
- Capitalize "I" and "RAG."
- "Wanna" is informal; "want to" fits technical writing.

Original:
> is my repotory, you can update my process to it

Natural version:
> This is my repository. You can use it to track my learning progress.

Notes:
- Spelling: "repository."
- "Progress" means how far you have advanced; "process" means a series of actions.

Original:
> and i will use english with you, you can finish my task and same time rectify my grammer and word

Natural version:
> I'll communicate with you in English. Please help me with my tasks and correct my grammar and word choice at the same time.

Notes:
- Use "at the same time."
- Spelling: "grammar."
- "Correct my grammar and word choice" is a natural expression.

## Five-minute session journal

Write 3-5 sentences:

- Today I learned...
- I implemented or tested...
- The result showed...
- I am still unsure about...
- Next, I will...

Keep the original and corrected versions when a correction teaches you something. Technical clarity matters more than perfect English.
